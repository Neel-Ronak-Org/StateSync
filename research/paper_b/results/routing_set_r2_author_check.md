**The two sources.** (1) A database of 23 standard annual figures (revenue, net income, total assets, cash flow, dividends, share repurchases, debt, EPS and so on) for 31 large US companies, fiscal years about 2015-2024. (2) A library of 84 annual and quarterly filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K) of the same companies, covering some years only.

**Classes.** docs: only the filings can answer (narrative, segments, quarters, notes, figures the database does not hold). either: the database holds every figure needed AND the matching filing is in the library. sql: only the database can answer in practice (across many companies, or many years). hybrid: needs both (a database figure plus what a filing says). neither: no source holds it.
