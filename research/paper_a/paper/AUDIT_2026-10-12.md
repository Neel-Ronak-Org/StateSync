## Corrections made to the paper

1. **Answer-level label defined.** H3's "2 of 242" uses the answer-text label (298 answers labelled, 242
   correct), which the paper never defined; 303 answers are correct by the SQL label.
2. **Rewording counts scoped.** "81 questions" is over all 498 Mini-Dev questions; on the 393 held-out
   questions the correction file changes the question or hint of 96 (59 questions, 51 hints).
3. **Matched-cost claim softened.** At one call each the judge's lead over self-consistency is
   significant for gpt-oss only (GPT-5 mini: CI -0.11 to 0.004). Abstract, contributions, results and
   conclusion now say so.
4. **How the study was pre-registered.** A version-controlled protocol with commits before the runs; no
   public registry. The three amendments made after the confirmatory results are named.
5. **Annotators are LLMs,** stated where they are first mentioned, not only in Limitations.
6. **Two checks act as well as warn** (a fan-out rewrite on the held-out set; the grounding repair): now
   described, with the rule that judges and grounding are scored on the answer as first written.
7. **Self-consistency's risk for failed queries** (no samples, risk 1; 4 of 393) is stated.
8. **Intervals** are said to cover the sampling of questions, not run-to-run variation.
9. **One rounded bound** in the cost table: 0.076, not 0.077 (earlier commit).
10. "Removes self-consistency's lead" became "closes the gap": the lead was never significant.

## Weaknesses that remain (none is hidden in the paper)

- **Power.** The checks fired on 17 and 22 answers. The paper can say "no advantage found", not "no
  advantage exists" (CI -6.3 to +2.2 catches).
- **H1 and H4 untestable;** only H3 is supported, and grounding fired on 5 answers.
- **No independent human annotation.** Both annotators are LLMs; the first also helped build the system
  and draft the paper. One author's review of 18 items was not blind.
- **Pre-registration is self-hosted.** Commit times are author-controlled evidence.
- **One benchmark, two models,** both possibly trained on BIRD; hosted through third parties.
- **Run-to-run variation** changes 10% of labels (5 of 50), about as large as the effects studied.
- **Labels depend on the date** for gold queries that use the current date (patients' ages); a re-score
  a week later changed none.
- **The judge reads the answer text,** so the host's empty texts (39 in the second model's run) lower
  its score; both figures are reported.
